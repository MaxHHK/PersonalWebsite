\section*{Biography}

Haokai Hong is currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), Hong Kong SAR, China, under the supervision of Prof. Kay Chen Tan, Professor at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Prior to joining PolyU, he received the Bachelor degree and Master degree from the School of Informatics, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China, in 2020 and 2023, respectively. His research interests include artificial intelligence for science, molecular dynamics simulation, geometric deep learning, generative modeling, and large-scale multiobjective optimization.
